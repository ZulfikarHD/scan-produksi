# Academic & Industrial Technical Writing (Skripsi & Operational System Documentation)

A comprehensive guide for authoring rigorous, narrative-driven technical thesis chapters (Skripsi), operational case studies, and software engineering documentation.

## Core Writing Philosophy

1. **Flowing Technical & Operational Storytelling:**
   - Avoid dry, disjointed bullet-point lists or generic prompt-like AI summaries.
   - Build deep operational context through connected narrative paragraphs that portray shop-floor realities, human workflows, machine operations, and administrative friction.
   - **Strictly Ban AI Slop, Rhetorical Grandstanding & Artificial Fluff:**
     - Never use AI clichés like *"bukan sekadar X, melainkan Y"*, *"bukan hanya X biasa, melainkan restrukturisasi fundamental"*, *"mengukir tonggak sejarah baru"*, or *"lompatan paradigma"*.
     - Avoid buzzword clusters (e.g., *"tingkat keandalan, stabilitas, dan keterpakaian"*).
     - State technical mechanisms, components, and factual workflows directly (*Show, don't preach*).
2. **Shop-Floor Operational Grounding:**
   - Ground every claim in real factory dynamics of PT Percetakan Dokumen Sekuriti (Unit Pengemasan, Karawang, Kartu Mesin Produksi, Barcode Code 128, SAP Production Order, 3 shifts: Shift 1, Shift 2, Shift 3, Petugas/Operator and Supervisor).
3. **Punctuation & Phrasing Standards:**
   - Do NOT use em-dashes ("—"). Use commas, parentheses, or natural Indonesian conjunctions (*"yaitu"*, *"seperti"*, *"antara lain"*).
   - Use **"NP (Nomor Pegawai)"** as the username credential for system users.
   - Use **"coding"** or **"pembuatan aplikasi"** instead of *"penulisan baris kode"* or *"pengkodean"*.
   - Use **"sebelum pembuatan aplikasi dimulai"** instead of *"sebelum pengkodean dimulai"*.
   - Use **"data hasil scanner"** instead of *"masukan scanner fisik"*.
   - Use **"dokumen hasil kerja"** / **"berkas spesifikasi"** instead of *"artefak dokumentasi"*.
   - Use **"acuan"** instead of *"tolok ukur"*.
   - Use **"Kepala Unit / Supervisor yang memeriksa laporan"** instead of *"penyelia laporan"*.
   - Use **"data yang valid dan akurat"** instead of *"data yang absolut"*.
4. **Numbered Point Structuring:**
   - When numbering items with titles, insert `<br>` after the bold title so the explanatory narrative paragraph starts neatly on the next line:
     ```markdown
     1. **Nama Poin / Tahapan**
        <br>
        Penjelasan komprehensif ditulis dalam bentuk paragraf mengalir di bawah judul...
     ```
